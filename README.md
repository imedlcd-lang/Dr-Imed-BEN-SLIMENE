## 🧭 Scientific Profile

I am a hydrogeologist and geospatial scientist specializing in **groundwater systems under environmental and structural constraints**. My research integrates:

- Hydrogeochemistry  
- Structural hydrogeology  
- GIS & remote sensing  
- Machine learning for water resource management  

With over a decade of academic and applied experience across **Tunisia, Germany, and Saudi Arabia**, my work aims to bridge **data-driven modelling with real-world hydrological challenges** in arid and semi-arid regions.

---

## 🔬 Research Vision

My research addresses a central question:

> *How can we integrate geospatial intelligence, hydrochemical signals, and machine learning to better understand and predict groundwater system behavior under climatic and anthropogenic stress?*

I develop hybrid methodologies combining:

- Remote sensing + lineament extraction (KDE)
- Machine learning (RF, ANN, KNN, R² > 0.90)
- Isotope hydrology (δ¹⁸O, δ²H, δ¹³C)
- Multivariate statistics (PCA, HCA)
